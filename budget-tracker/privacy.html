Privacy policy

Budget Tracker · Last updated 24 November 2026

The short version

Budget Tracker keeps everything you enter on your own device. We don't collect, store, sell or share any of your data, because none of it ever reaches us.

What the app stores, and where

The transactions (amount, type, category, date and note) and monthly budgets you enter are saved only in the app's storage on your device. The app has no account or sign-in and runs no servers.

App lock

If you turn on the optional app lock, Android checks your fingerprint, face or phone PIN and only tells the app whether the check passed. The app never sees or stores your biometric data or PIN. The on/off setting is saved on your device.

What the app sends over the internet

Nothing. The app makes no network requests. It contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting code.

Backups and files you export

  • When you save a backup or export a CSV, the file is created on your device and handed to the place you choose, such as Files, Google Drive or email. After that it is governed by that service's own terms.
  • Android may include the app's data in your device's own backup to your Google account, if you have turned that on in Android settings. That backup belongs to your Google account; we can't see it.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes everything it saved. There is no copy anywhere else unless you made a backup.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If a future version changes any of this, for example by adding optional cloud sync, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
